My Day Buddy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

The short version: My Day Buddy has no account system, no server and no analytics. Everything you type stays in your own browser, on your own device. Nobody — including the developer — can read it.

What My Day Buddy stores, and where

All of your entries — tasks, habits, goals, focus sessions, journal text, mood, health logs and menstrual cycle data — are written to localStorage in the browser or app on the device you are using. Journal photos and voice notes are stored in IndexedDB on the same device. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

Because the data is tied to one app on one device, it does not sync between your phone and your computer, and it is erased if you clear the app's data, use a private browsing window, or uninstall the app.

What is never collected

  • No account, email address, name or phone number is sent anywhere. The name you enter is only shown back to you.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no cookies used for tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties, because no data reaches the developer in the first place.

Third parties that see a network request

My Day Buddy is built with web technology, so opening it involves ordinary web requests for the app's own files. These parties can see your IP address and that a file was requested, but never your entries:

  • GitHub Pages — hosts the app's files.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — supplies the typefaces.
  • unpkg — supplies the icon library.

Once loaded, the app works fully offline and makes no further requests.

Sensitive categories

My Day Buddy lets you record health-related information, including mood, sleep, medicines and — if you turn it on — menstrual cycle data. This is treated exactly like every other entry: written to your device only, never uploaded, never analysed by anyone but you. Menstrual cycle tracking is off by default and can be switched off at any time in Settings.

My Day Buddy is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Cycle predictions are estimates calculated from your own logged data. They are not a contraceptive method. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Permissions the app may ask for

All are optional, and My Day Buddy works without any of them. Each is requested only at the moment a feature needs it, and can be withdrawn at any time in your device or browser settings.

  • Notifications — to show the reminders you switch on. These are generated by the app itself and only appear while My Day Buddy is open.
  • Microphone — only while you are recording a voice note in the Journal. The recording is saved on your device.
  • Persistent storage — asks your browser not to automatically delete My Day Buddy's data when the device is low on space.
  • Device unlock (fingerprint or face) — only if you choose to lock your Journal. Your device performs the check; My Day Buddy never receives your biometric data.

Your control over the data

  • Export — Settings → Data → Export JSON gives you a complete copy of everything.
  • Delete — Settings → Data → Reset All Data erases it permanently. Clearing the app's storage does the same.
  • There is nothing to request from the developer, because the developer holds nothing.

Children

My Day Buddy is not directed at children under 13, and no data is knowingly collected from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, you will be asked to review it the next time you open the app. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
